Our story

A café made to feel like a little escape.

Garden Rose is a maid café concept inspired by delicate gardens, classic café culture and the joy of being treated like an honored guest. Our goal is to create a beautiful, comfortable place where people can enjoy delicious food, share moments with friends, celebrate special occasions and experience the playful charm of maid café hospitality.

Whether you visit for a quiet dessert, a themed event or simply a memorable afternoon, Garden Rose is designed to feel welcoming from the moment you enter.
